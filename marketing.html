Why RISE

What RISE Offers Your Institution

A turnkey financial literacy program you can add to your customer portal without building, buying, or maintaining anything.

RISE turns financial literacy into a benefit you offer your customers — no curriculum to build, no content to maintain, no IT integration required. Your customers log in to a portal branded with your logo and colors, learn the essentials at their own pace, and arrive at your loan officer's desk with the knowledge to qualify.

No app to install. No spreadsheets. No homework. Just clear, plain-language tools that meet people where they are.

Everything is hosted, updated, and supported by LaunchPoint. You point your customers at a URL; we handle the rest.

  • Branded portal A custom subdomain like yourname.launchpointrise.com, painted in your logo and colors.
  • 15+ modules Credit, paychecks, budgeting, loans, banking, taxes, benefits, and more.
  • English & Spanish Every module ships bilingual; users switch with one click.
  • Auto-save progress Customers come back to where they left off — from any device.

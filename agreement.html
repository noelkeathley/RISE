RISE Partner License Agreement

This License Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between LaunchPoint RISE (“Provider”), and the partner organization identified below (“Customer”), effective as of the date of Customer’s first payment (“Effective Date”).

1. Services

1.1Provider operates LaunchPoint RISE, a software-as-a-service (SaaS) financial literacy education platform delivered via the internet (the “Platform”).
1.2During the term of this Agreement, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to access the Platform via a Customer-specific branded URL (the “Customer URL”) provided by Provider, and to make the Platform available to Customer’s end users.
1.3Provider will customize the Customer URL with Customer’s logo, brand colors, welcome message, and contact information, as supplied by Customer.

2. License Fee and Payment

2.1Customer shall pay Provider the monthly license fee set forth in Schedule A (the “License Fee”), billed in advance on a monthly basis.
2.2Invoices are due Net 15 from the invoice date.
2.3Late payments may result in suspension of Customer’s access to the Platform, including deactivation of the Customer URL, until payment is received.

3. Permitted Use

3.1 Customer may:
  1. Link to the Customer URL from Customer’s owned digital properties (website, mobile app, email, statements);
  2. Share the Customer URL with Customer’s customers, members, employees, or other intended end users;
  3. Reference the Platform in Customer’s marketing materials as a benefit provided to Customer’s customers.
3.2 Customer may not:
  1. Repackage, white-label, sublicense, or redistribute the Platform or its content outside Customer’s owned digital properties without Provider’s prior written consent;
  2. Modify, copy, reverse engineer, or create derivative works from the Platform;
  3. Use the Platform in any way that violates applicable laws or regulations;
  4. Use the Platform to make specific financial recommendations or provide financial advice to end users beyond the educational content provided by Provider.

4. Content, Intellectual Property, and Branding

4.1Provider retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform, including all educational content, software, designs, and underlying intellectual property.
4.2Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to Customer’s logo, brand assets, and any content Customer provides to Provider for inclusion in the Customer URL. Customer grants Provider a limited license to use such assets solely to provide the Services.
4.3Provider may identify Customer by name and logo on Provider’s website and marketing materials as a partner, unless Customer requests in writing not to be identified.

5. Data, Privacy, and Analytics

5.1The Platform does not collect personally identifiable financial information from end users. The Platform may collect anonymized usage data (page views, session length, content engagement) for analytics purposes.
5.2Provider will provide Customer with periodic engagement reports describing aggregate, anonymized usage of the Customer URL.
5.3The Platform is educational in nature. The Platform is not a financial product, does not provide individualized financial, legal, or tax advice, and is not regulated as a financial institution. Provider does not collect, store, or process any consumer payment information or sensitive financial account information.

6. Service Level and Availability

6.1Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain Platform availability of at least 99% per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
6.2Provider may update or modify the Platform from time to time. Provider will notify Customer of any material changes to the Platform’s content or functionality.

7. Term and Termination

7.1This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues on a month-to-month basis.
7.2Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason with thirty (30) days written notice to the other party.
7.3 Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Customer:
  1. Fails to pay an invoice more than thirty (30) days past due;
  2. Materially breaches Section 3 (Permitted Use) of this Agreement;
  3. Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.
7.4Upon termination, Customer’s access to the Customer URL will be deactivated. Customer remains responsible for any unpaid License Fees accrued through the termination date. No refunds will be issued for partial months.

8. Confidentiality

8.1Each party agrees to keep confidential any non-public business or technical information shared by the other party in connection with this Agreement.
8.2This Section 8 does not apply to information that is publicly known, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

9.2EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE PLATFORM IS PROVIDED “AS IS” WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION ANY WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT.
9.3The Platform provides educational content only. Customer acknowledges and agrees that the Platform does not constitute financial, legal, tax, or investment advice, and Customer will not represent it as such to its end users.

10. Limitation of Liability

10.1TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING FROM THIS AGREEMENT.
10.2PROVIDER’S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER TO PROVIDER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

11.1This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes any prior agreements.
11.2This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

RISE is a white-labeled financial literacy platform that banks, credit unions, and auto dealers can offer to their customers as a free educational resource.

Why partners offer RISE to their customers:

  • Higher loan approval rates. Customers who understand credit walk in with stronger applications.

  • Deeper customer loyalty. Being the institution that helped them get ready builds lifelong trust.
  • Community impact. Demonstrate real commitment to financial inclusion — useful for CRA compliance, marketing, and community relations.
  • Zero lift on your end. RISE is fully hosted, branded to match your site, and updated for you.

Your customers become more loan-ready. You become the institution that helped them get there.
